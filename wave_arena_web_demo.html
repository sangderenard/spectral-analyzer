Reading the field

Power is light-field intensity: bright places carry more energy. The beam can spread and interfere even when total field power stays near 1.00000.

Relative phase is the wave's local timing. Its rainbow colours mark angle in the complex plane, not the light's visible colour. Watch bands bend where the emitters overlap.

Change compares the current complex field with the starting field. It includes phase change, so it can glow where power appears similar.

Ways to play

Try Single beam diffraction to see spreading without a second source. Then load Close coherent pair and adjust their relative phase: the interference pattern moves. Increase distance per step to advance farther each tick; increase travel range to let the field evolve longer before reversing. Pixel pitch changes the physical width and sampling of the arena.
